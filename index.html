About Eipi

We turn wild ideas into signs that refuse to be ignored.

We started in a small workshop in Purmerend with a cutting table and no clients. Not much has changed except the workshop. Eipi still runs on the same rule: if a sign doesn't get noticed, it goes back to the shop.

Today the workshop ships facade letters, fleet wraps and wayfinding across the Netherlands and Europe — still one team, still zero middlemen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before we cut.

Everything you need to know regarding fleet downtime, municipal permits, warranties, and file preparation.

A single commercial van typically takes 1 to 2 business days from thorough surface de-greasing to final post-heating. For multi-vehicle fleets (e.g. 10–20 delivery vans), we organize rolling installation schedules or weekend shifts so your fleet never suffers operational downtime.

Yes. We measure the facade, calculate light values according to local zoning guidelines (welstandseisen), generate the technical architectural drawings, and handle the environmental permit application (omgevingsvergunning) with the municipality on your behalf.

Absolutely. We exclusively apply premium cast films from 3M and Avery Dennison engineered with clean-release adhesives. When removed professionally in our workshop with controlled infrared heat, factory paintwork remains 100% intact, protected against stone chips and sun bleaching.

All vehicle wraps come with an unconditional 5-year guarantee covering adhesion, bubble prevention, and UV discoloration. For exterior facade lettering, we provide 5 years warranty on all aluminum structures, powder coatings, and certified high-efficiency LED modules.
